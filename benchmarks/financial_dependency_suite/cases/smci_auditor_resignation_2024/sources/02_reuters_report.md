# Reuters report on EY resignation

Source: Reuters
Published: 2024-10-30

Reuters reported that Ernst & Young resigned as Super Micro Computer's
auditor.

According to Super Micro's SEC filing, EY resigned after receiving
additional information during the review process. EY concluded that it
could no longer rely on representations from management and the Audit
Committee and was unwilling to remain associated with management's
financial statements.

Super Micro disagreed with EY's decision.
