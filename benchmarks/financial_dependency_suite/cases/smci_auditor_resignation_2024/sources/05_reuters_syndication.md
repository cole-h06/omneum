# Syndicated Reuters report on EY resignation

Source: ThePrint / Reuters
Published: 2024-10-30

This report was supplied by Reuters.

Reuters reported that Ernst & Young resigned as Super Micro Computer's
auditor. The report states that EY decided to resign after receiving
additional information during the review process.

It reports that Super Micro's SEC filing said EY could no longer rely
on representations from management and the Audit Committee and was
unwilling to remain associated with management's financial statements.
