# Super Micro Computer Form 8-K

Source: Super Micro Computer, Inc.
Type: SEC Form 8-K
Filed: 2024-10-30
Period of report: 2024-10-24

Super Micro disclosed that Ernst & Young resigned as its independent
registered public accounting firm on October 24, 2024.

EY said recently obtained information meant it could no longer rely on
representations from management and the Audit Committee. EY also said
it was unwilling to remain associated with financial statements
prepared by management and could no longer provide audit services
consistent with its professional obligations.

Super Micro stated that it disagreed with EY's decision.
