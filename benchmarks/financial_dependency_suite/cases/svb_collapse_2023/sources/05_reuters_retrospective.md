# Reuters — How SVB Financial Group Imploded

Source: Reuters
Document type: Retrospective timeline

Reuters reconstructed the sequence of events surrounding the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank.

On March 8, 2023, SVB announced that it intended to raise approximately $2.25 billion through common equity and preferred convertible stock.

The announcement followed the sale of a portfolio of U.S. Treasury and mortgage-backed securities at an approximately $1.8 billion loss.

On March 9, customers withdrew approximately $42 billion from the bank as concerns intensified.

On March 10, California regulators closed Silicon Valley Bank and appointed the FDIC as receiver.

The retrospective identifies SVB's March 8 disclosure of the securities loss and capital raise as the beginning of the two-day sequence that culminated in the bank's closure.
