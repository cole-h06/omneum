# SVB Financial Group — Capital Offerings Press Release

Source: SVB Financial Group
Published: March 8, 2023
Document type: Press release
SEC exhibit: 99.1
Parent filing: 0001193125-23-064680

SVB Financial Group announced that it intended to offer $1.25 billion of common stock and $500 million of mandatory convertible preferred stock.

Before commencing the offerings, SVB also entered into an agreement with General Atlantic for a $500 million common-stock investment at the public offering price.

The proposed transactions therefore contemplated approximately $2.25 billion of new capital.

This press release was furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to SVB Financial Group's March 8, 2023 Form 8-K.
