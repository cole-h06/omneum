# SVB Financial Group — Strategic Actions / Q1 2023 Mid-Quarter Update

Source: SVB Financial Group
Published: March 8, 2023
Document type: Investor presentation
SEC exhibit: 99.2
Parent filing: 0001193125-23-064680

SVB Financial Group described strategic actions intended to strengthen its financial position and increase balance-sheet flexibility.

SVB reported that it sold substantially all of its available-for-sale securities portfolio.

The presentation reported an AFS sale size of approximately $21 billion and a preliminary estimated realized loss of approximately $1.8 billion after tax.

SVB said it intended to reinvest the proceeds and was seeking approximately $2.25 billion through common equity, mandatory convertible preferred shares, and General Atlantic's committed investment.

The presentation states that the $21 billion AFS securities sale was executed on March 8, 2023.

This presentation was furnished as Exhibit 99.2 to SVB Financial Group's March 8, 2023 Form 8-K.
