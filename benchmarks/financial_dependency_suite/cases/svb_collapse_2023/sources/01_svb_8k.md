# SVB Financial Group — Form 8-K

Source: SVB Financial Group
Filed: March 8, 2023
Document type: SEC Form 8-K
Accession: 0001193125-23-064680

SVB Financial Group announced capital-raising actions and provided an update on strategic actions taken to strengthen its financial position.

SVB reported that it had completed the sale of substantially all of its available-for-sale securities portfolio on March 8, 2023.

The company stated that the AFS portfolio sale would result in an approximately $1.8 billion after-tax loss in the first quarter.

The filing incorporated SVB's March 8 press release, mid-quarter strategic update, and investor letter as exhibits.
