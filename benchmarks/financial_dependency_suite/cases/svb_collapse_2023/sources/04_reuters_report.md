# Reuters — Silicon Valley Bank Capital Raise

Source: Reuters
Published: March 9, 2023
Document type: News report

Reuters reported that SVB Financial Group was selling assets and raising capital to strengthen its balance sheet.

SVB launched a $1.75 billion share sale after selling a roughly $21 billion bond portfolio.

The sale of that portfolio produced an approximately $1.8 billion loss.

Reuters attributed the balance-sheet restructuring, asset sale, and capital-raising information to SVB's disclosures while also reporting additional market reaction and information from its own sources.

SVB shares fell sharply following the announcement.
